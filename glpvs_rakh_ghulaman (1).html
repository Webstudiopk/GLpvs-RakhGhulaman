Our Institution

Excellence in Education

GLPVS Rakh Ghulaman is a Government-run institution serving the community of Rakh Ghulaman, Bhakkar District in Punjab, Pakistan. We are committed to providing quality education and vocational training to the youth of the region.

Located on Rakh Ghulaman Rd, Chak 22 ML, our institution has been a cornerstone of educational development in the area — nurturing generations of students with knowledge, discipline, and character.

We offer a safe, supportive learning environment with dedicated staff, modern facilities, and a canteen & hostel for students from distant areas.
